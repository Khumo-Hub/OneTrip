## Editorial rules

1. Every public guide names its scope and links to the official institution.
2. Verify change-prone process information at least weekly, other guidance monthly, and any reported material change immediately.
3. If current information is uncertain, remove the assertion or present the official-check step.
4. Publish only after a named human content reviewer has signed off.
5. Do not claim to book appointments, guarantee successful visits, act for institutions or handle their payments.
6. Do not collect or transmit ID numbers, financial credentials, health information or scanned documents.

## Outstanding before public release

- Verify all eight guides' detailed wording, exceptions and source links on the final publication date.

- Decide domain and deployment platform; establish canonical URLs and sitemap only after that decision.
- Complete manual visual, accessibility and functional QA including real-mobile checks.
- Run a pilot with everyday users and capture feedback without leaking sensitive data.
